## What you get on an approved inward

Qty in the document table is editable, owner only. Type a new number and
the field turns amber:

    This document is approved, so 6 pieces are already in stock and on
    the vendor's bill. Changing it to 5 adjusts the stock by -1 and
    posts the difference to what you owe, dated today.

One reason, and three things move together: stock (its own adjustment
document), the line, and the vendor payable (a delta journal dated
today). Refused if any payment has been allocated, or if the pieces have
already sold or moved.

The Document card also shows, when the books have drifted:

    The books are behind this document. kanhai jewels is posted at
    Rs3,915.00, but the document now works out to Rs4,847.00 -- a
    difference of Rs932.00 understated.   [Post the difference]

Nothing renders when they agree, which is almost always.

## Why the editor was unreachable

On an approved inward the page renders InwardDocTable (read-only) and
PricingPanel. LinesSection is the only thing that mounted LineQtyEditor,
and it renders only when editable={isDraft}. So the whole correction path
-- function, trigger exemption, delta journal, paid-bill lock -- was live
and had no way in.

InwardDocTable now carries "use client" and takes two new optional props,
inwardId and canCorrectQty. It has no server-only imports, so the
directive is safe.
